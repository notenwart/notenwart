# Getting started

notenwart runs in the browser – on a phone, a tablet or a computer. Nothing has to be installed; the address comes
from your school or ensemble (for example `https://noten.example.org`).

## Your first sign-in

You get an **invitation link** – by email, or handed over by whoever looks after notenwart at your organisation. It
works once and for seven days. Open it, choose a password (at least 10 characters) and you are in. If the link has
run out, ask for a new one; a new link replaces the old one.

## Signing in later

- **Email and password** on the sign-in page.
- **No password yet, or forgotten it?** Enter your email address and choose *Email me a sign-in link*. The link
  works once, for 20 minutes. (Only when your server sends email; otherwise ask your admin for a new link.)
- **With your school account:** if your organisation has set it up, the sign-in page shows a button such as
  *Sign in with Microsoft 365*. This only works for people who already have an account in notenwart, with the same
  email address.

You stay signed in on a device for 30 days after your last visit. *Sign out* (top right, or in the menu on narrow
screens) removes everything notenwart stored on the device, including music saved for offline use.

## As an app on your home screen

notenwart can be added to the home screen like an app: in Safari on iPhone and iPad *Share → Add to Home Screen*, in
Chrome on Android *⋮ → Add to Home screen* or *Install app*. It then opens without the browser's bars.

## Your account

Your name (top right) opens your account page:

| Setting | |
|---|---|
| **Name** and **password** | the name others see; a new password needs the current one |
| **Picture** | a profile picture, if your organisation allows them – otherwise your initials are shown |
| **Language** | German or English; until you choose, notenwart follows the browser |
| **Colours** | light, dark or as on this device (also with the button in the header); remembered per device |
| **Notifications** | which messages you get, and how: in the app, by email, push (coming later) |
| **Your data** | *Download my data* gives you everything notenwart stores about you, as a file |

## Notifications

The bell in the header shows how many messages are new. notenwart tells you about:

- new parts for you – a new part, a new work for your ensemble, or a single part given to you,
- a programme that was created or changed,
- a printed part you borrowed that is due tomorrow or overdue,
- markings a leader shared,
- (leaders) a guest link that runs out tomorrow.

On your account page you choose for each kind whether it appears in the app, comes by email, or both. Emails collect
everything of a few minutes into one message. Nobody is told about what they did themselves.
