# Playing and practising

## My music

*My music* is the start page. It lists, for each of your ensembles:

- what you play there (*You play:* e.g. "Trumpet 2"),
- the ensemble's **programmes** – concerts or set lists – with your parts in playing order,
- **all works** of the ensemble with your parts.

If you have borrowed printed music from the archive, it is listed at the top with the date to return it by.

Tap a part to open it. Nothing there yet? Your leader has not given the ensemble any works, or has not yet said
which instrument you play – ask them.

## Reading in the viewer

The viewer shows the part full screen; the screen stays on while it is open.

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| turn forward | tap the right third, swipe left, or press →, Page Down, Space or Enter |
| turn back | tap the left third, swipe right, or press ←, Page Up |
| show the controls | tap the middle (they hide again after a few seconds) |
| close | *Close* (×) at the top |

**Page turners:** Bluetooth pedals that send arrow keys, Page Up/Down or Space work as they are.

**Page layout** (in the controls):

- *1 page* – one page at a time.
- *Half turns* – the top half already shows the next page while the bottom half still shows the end of the current
  one; a dashed line marks the seam. You keep reading without a jump.
- *2 pages* – two pages side by side; the default on wide screens in landscape.

The layout is remembered per device. Turning past the last page opens the next piece – of the programme you came from,
or of the ensemble – and turning back before the first page opens the previous one at its end.

## Offline

Switch on **Available offline** for an ensemble on *My music*. All your parts of that ensemble are then stored on the
device; each time you open notenwart with a connection, new and changed parts are fetched and parts you no longer
have are removed. Without a connection, *My music* and the viewer work from the stored copies.

The device forgets them when you sign out. Recordings and shared markings need a connection.

## Writing in your part

*Annotate* in the viewer opens the tools: **pen** (three colours), **highlighter**, **eraser** and **undo**; *Done*
closes them. You write on whole pages (not while a half turn is shown). Your markings are yours alone: saved on the
device at once and on the server shortly after, also when you were offline. On another device you see the same
markings; if you write on two devices at once, the later one counts.

The PDF itself never changes – markings lie on top of it.

**Markings of your leaders:** when a leader marks a part for the whole ensemble (bowings, breaths, cuts …), you see
these markings beneath your own. You cannot change them; *Leaders' markings* in the viewer hides or shows them.

## Printing

*Print / PDF* in the viewer opens a copy of the part with your markings – and the leaders', when they are shown –
drawn in. Print it or save it as a PDF.

## Practising with recordings

Recordings belong to a work: **full recordings** of the whole piece, and **practice recordings** of a single part or
voice (for example the alto part of a choir piece). They can be recorded or generated, for example from MIDI.

When a work has recordings, the viewer shows a player bar beneath the music; your own part's practice recordings come
first. You can

- choose the recording,
- play and pause, jump 5 seconds back or ahead, move to any position,
- play **slower or faster** (0.5× to 1.25×) – the pitch stays the same,
- repeat a passage: *A–B* sets the start, pressing again sets the end, once more stops repeating.
