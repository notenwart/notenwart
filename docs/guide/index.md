# User guide

notenwart knows four roles. Everyone starts with [Getting started]({{ '/guide/getting-started/' | relative_url }});
the other pages build on each other – a leader can do everything a player can, the library everything a leader can,
and admins everything.

| Role | Does | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Member** (pupil, player, singer) | sees and plays their own parts, practises with recordings, writes in their parts | [Playing and practising]({{ '/guide/playing/' | relative_url }}) |
| **Leader** (teacher, conductor) | looks after the ensembles they lead: players, works, programmes, guest links, shared markings | [Leading an ensemble]({{ '/guide/leading/' | relative_url }}) |
| **Librarian** | keeps the library: works, PDFs, parts, imports, instruments, the paper archive | [Looking after the library]({{ '/guide/library/' | relative_url }}) |
| **Admin** | sets up the organisation, adds people, keeps an eye on the system | [Administration]({{ '/guide/admin/' | relative_url }}) |

The role belongs to the person; *leading* belongs to an ensemble. A teacher with the role leader leads only the
ensembles an admin or librarian has made them lead.

**Who sees which part:** a member sees a part when one of their ensembles has the work and they play that part's
instrument there – "Trumpet" without a number means every trumpet part, "Trumpet 2" just the second. A part
without an instrument (the full score) is for the leaders. Leaders can give single members single extra parts.
